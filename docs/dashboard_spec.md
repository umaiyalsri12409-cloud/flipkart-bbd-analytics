\# Big Billion Days Dashboard Specification



\## 1. Dashboard Purpose

The purpose of this dashboard is to analyze Flipkart Big Billion Days sales performance, revenue, product categories, regions, and sales representative performance.



\## 2. Data Source

\- File: `data/orders\_enriched.csv`

\- Source: SQL query `q09`

\- Expected records: 90 data rows, excluding the header.

\- The dataset contains order details along with customer, product, and representative information.



\## 3. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard will display the following KPIs:



1\. \*\*Total Completed Orders:\*\* Count of orders with status `completed`.

2\. \*\*Total Revenue:\*\* Sum of `amount` for completed orders only.

3\. \*\*Average Order Value:\*\* Total completed-order revenue divided by the number of completed orders.

4\. \*\*Revenue by Region:\*\* Total completed-order revenue for each region.

5\. \*\*Revenue by Product Category:\*\* Total completed-order revenue for each product category.

6\. \*\*Representative Performance:\*\* Revenue generated by each representative compared with their monthly target.



\## 4. Charts and Visualizations

\- \*\*Bar Chart – Revenue by Region:\*\* Compares revenue across different regions.

\- \*\*Bar Chart – Revenue by Product Category:\*\* Shows which product categories generate the most revenue.

\- \*\*Line Chart – Monthly Revenue:\*\* Displays revenue trends over time, if the dataset contains suitable date information.

\- \*\*Bar Chart – Representative Performance:\*\* Compares each representative's revenue with their monthly target.



\## 5. Dashboard Filters

Users should be able to filter the dashboard by:

\- Order month

\- Region

\- Product category

\- Sales representative

\- Order status



\## 6. Business Questions

The dashboard should help answer:

\- Which region generates the highest revenue?

\- Which product category performs best?

\- How does revenue change over time?

\- Which representatives meet or exceed their targets?

\- How many orders are completed, pending, or disputed?



\## 7. Data Validation

\- Include only completed orders when calculating revenue.

\- Exclude pending and disputed orders from completed-order revenue.

\- Check dashboard totals against the SQL query results.

\- Verify calculations using the spreadsheet analysis.

\- Investigate any differences before presenting the results.



\## 8. Expected Outcome

The dashboard should provide a clear overview of sales performance and help identify high-performing regions, product categories, and representatives.



Final recommendations should be added only after analyzing the actual query results.
